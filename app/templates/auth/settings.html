Privacy

Who can see your contact details

Lost & Found now connects people directly: a lost report is broadcast to the whole campus, and anyone who finds it notifies you with their own contact details. Your name and number are shown publicly on your reports so a finder can reach you.

Show my contact on my Lost & Found reportsOn: your phone is visible to anyone viewing your reports, so finders can contact you straight away. Off: it is hidden and coordination goes through campus administration.
Your email, USN and profile page are never exposed to other students — only the contact number above, and never to strangers or visitors.
